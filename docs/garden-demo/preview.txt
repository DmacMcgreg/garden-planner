# You can walk around the planting plan and watch the light change.
What this is: The planner models a backyard in feet and estimates sunlight at each bed.
The problem: A fresh launch used to show an empty lawn with the configuration controls open.
Why now: A new garden opens with four editable beds and a sunlight simulator beside them.
Words to know first:
- Backyard garden: The garden opens in 3D orbit. Each planting card opens its bed editor.
- Sunlight simulator: The curve follows the selected date and location. Its marker drives the light in the scene.
- Yard and photo: Compare the original backyard photo with the live render and calibrate its dimensions.
