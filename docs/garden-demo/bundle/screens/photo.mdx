In **Render**, choose the camera button to add a backyard photo. To estimate depth, enter the measured width and mark four corners of a rectangular ground patch. You can enter both measurements directly.

If the image contains GPS coordinates, **Use photo location** applies them and suggests the nearest listed city's time zone. You can choose a city yourself. The comparison slider places the original photo beside the render. The starter planting is editable and does not infer a custom crop layout from the photo.
